The problem

A long provider list is not a payment strategy.

The wrong introduction wastes compliance time on both sides. We qualify the context first, then open only the conversations that have a credible reason to move forward.

01

Generic lists ignore the operating reality

A provider may support your country on paper and still be the wrong fit for your vertical, traffic, methods or expected volume.

02

Cold outreach creates slow, low-signal conversations

Merchants repeat the same profile to multiple teams. PSPs spend time qualifying requests that should never have reached sales.

03

Approval is only the beginning

Commercial terms, integration path, payout logic and operational support matter just as much as the initial account opening.

The matching desk

Context first. Introductions second.

Questions

What happens after you send the brief?

Is OfferPSP itself a payment processor?

No. OfferPSP is a B2B matching and introduction service. Payment services, underwriting, onboarding and final commercial terms are provided by independent PSPs.

Do you publish your PSP partner list?

No. The network is not used as a public directory. We share relevant names only when there is a plausible fit for the specific merchant profile.

Can you guarantee approval?

No responsible intermediary can. Each provider runs its own due diligence and risk assessment. Our role is to reduce obviously mismatched conversations before that process begins.

What information should we prepare?

Entity and licensing status, website, target GEOs, vertical, payment methods, expected volume, average ticket, chargeback profile and any current processing constraints.

Which businesses do you work with?

We review e-commerce, online marketplaces, SaaS and subscription services, video game platforms and publishers, digital entertainment, esports and other cross-border businesses where relevant provider coverage exists.
