## Commercial objective

The first contact must produce a usable supplier relationship, not merely a generic partner
registration. OfferPSP requests five connected deliverables:

1. a current offer, coverage or product matrix that can be used for private matching;
2. merchant qualification criteria and the correct contracting entity;
3. OfferPSP remuneration: recurring revenue share, a fixed activation fee, permitted markup or a
   documented combination;
4. lead registration, attribution, protection and non-circumvention rules;
5. a named process and update cadence for keeping route and commercial information current.

Final merchant availability and pricing remain subject to provider review. OfferPSP must not
publish a provider identity, claim a guaranteed approval or represent indicative terms as final.

## Recording a response

Do not store one blended percentage as “the offer”. Record separately:

- provider/source merchant pricing and whether it is indicative or approved;
- OfferPSP recurring revenue share and its calculation base;
- fixed referral or activation fee and triggering event;
- permitted OfferPSP markup, if explicitly allowed;
- payout schedule, currency, minimum payout and clawback conditions;
- lead protection period and non-circumvention language;
- partner terms version, source and verification date;
- every atomic route as a versioned offer, not permanent provider-wide pricing.
