Privacy Notice

Effective date: 30 July 2026

Who we are

OfferPSP is a B2B payment-partner matching and introduction service operated as a Brain Index product. This notice covers information submitted through offerpsp.com.

Information we collect

When you submit a matching request, we collect the contact and business information you provide, including your name, work email, company, website, Telegram username, vertical, target markets, expected volume, required payment methods and request details.

How we use it

  • To assess whether OfferPSP can assist with your request.
  • To contact you about relevant payment-provider introductions.
  • To maintain a record of the request and prevent duplicate or abusive submissions.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

Sharing

We do not publish merchant profiles. Information is shared with an independent payment provider only when it is relevant to a potential introduction. We may use infrastructure providers required to host the website, automate the intake process and securely store submissions.

Retention and security

We retain a request only for as long as reasonably necessary to evaluate it, manage introductions and meet legal or operational requirements. Access to submitted profiles is restricted to the business workflow used for private review.

Your choices

You may ask us to correct or delete your submitted information, subject to applicable legal retention requirements. Contact us through Telegram and identify the email address used in the request.

Updates

We may update this notice as the service evolves. The current effective date will always appear at the top of this page.
